PAPERPIN — INSTALL ON YOUR MAC
For Intel and Apple silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later.

1. Drag Paperpin onto the Applications folder in this window.
   If you are updating, quit Paperpin first and choose Replace.
2. Eject the Paperpin disk image.
3. Open Paperpin from Applications.

FIRST OPEN — FREE BUILD
This app is locally signed and has not been notarized by Apple.
macOS may ask you to approve it once:

  Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway beside Paperpin.
  Confirm Open, then open Paperpin normally in the future.

Only approve the copy you intended to download from the project's release.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

SHARING WITH ANOTHER MAC
Send the release download LINK, not the DMG as a chat attachment.
On the receiving Mac, open that link in Safari and download the DMG there.
Some chat apps add a restrictive transfer flag to attachments. This can
cause “The application Paperpin can't be opened” with no Open Anyway button.

If this happens, eject the disk image, remove only the blocked app from
Applications, and download a fresh DMG through Safari using the release link.
Repeat the installation steps above. Keep your Paperpin data folder.
On managed Macs, your administrator may need to allow the app.

YOUR NOTES
Updates keep your existing notes on this Mac. The installer contains no
personal notes and does not transfer notes from another Mac.

Liquid Glass is available only on macOS 26+ with native support. Older Macs
use the opaque paper appearance and show a disabled Liquid Glass option.
Choose Paperpin > Launch at Login if you want it to start automatically.
